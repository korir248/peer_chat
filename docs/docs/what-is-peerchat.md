# What is peerChat?

peerChat is a desktop chat application that connects you directly to other people with no server in the middle.

Most chat apps — even "encrypted" ones — route your messages through a central server. That server can go down, get blocked, get hacked, or get subpoenaed.

peerChat has no server. Your messages go from your device directly to theirs.

## Two modes

### Local network

On the same Wi-Fi or LAN, peerChat automatically discovers anyone else running the app. No configuration, no IP addresses — it just works.

### Anywhere in the world

Share your public key with anyone and chat directly, peer-to-peer, regardless of where you are. No account needed, no middleman.

## Who is it for?

- Anyone who values privacy
- Teams in environments with unreliable internet
- Healthcare workers who can't put patient data on third-party servers
- Anyone in a region where communication infrastructure is unreliable or surveilled
